Why it looks different

A concrete example from the data: an annual software subscription of 12,000 kr., paid in January.

Cash basis

12.000 kr.

The full amount is deducted in January — the one month the money left the account. January's result looks artificially off.

→

Periodized

1.000 kr./mo.

The amount is spread across the 12 months the subscription covers (Jan–Dec). Each month only carries the expense it actually owes.
